title=Third Person Camera for Project Viewpoint
description=[h1]What it does[/h1]
description=
description=Turns Project Viewpoint's third-person view into a full camera, on foot and in vehicles.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Over the shoulder[/b]: smoothed follow, left or right shoulder with a swap key, mouse wheel distance.
description=[*][b]Indoors[/b]: the camera moves in under a roof and eases back out after a wall pushed it in.
description=[*][b]Combat[/b]: the combat stance backs off to keep both flanks in view, aiming a firearm moves in over the shoulder.
description=[*][b]Chase camera[/b]: the camera has weight. It lags when you speed up, closes in when you brake, drifts wide in turns and swings in behind where you are going, slides included.
description=[*][b]Speed feel[/b]: the faster you go, the further it drops back and the wider the view, scaled to each vehicle's top speed. One slider, 0 fixes the camera to the vehicle.
description=[*][b]Sized to the vehicle[/b]: distance and height follow the vehicle, from bike to fire truck, trailers included.
description=[*][b]Smooth mouse[/b] while seated.
description=[*][b]Cursor key[/b]: a keyboard key for Viewpoint's cursor toggle, in place of the middle mouse button.
description=[*][b]Controller[/b]: the right stick turns the camera, details below.
description=[/list]
description=
description=Settings in Viewpoint's settings window under [b]Third person[/b], keys under [b]Keys → Third person camera[/b], controller settings under [b]Third person → Controller[/b].
description=
description=[h1]Controller[/h1]
description=Works with the game's own controller layout, on Xbox, PlayStation and Steam Deck controllers. Button names below are Xbox, the table has the others. While Viewpoint's view is on:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Switching views[/b]: hold Back, the button that opens the game's own radial menu, pick the entry with the right stick and let go. Viewpoint on or off, first or third person, swap shoulder.
description=[*][b]Right stick[/b] turns the camera, on foot and in vehicles. The game aims with it otherwise, so you aim with a light pull on RT, as in the game's Default preset, or with LT in the preset below. The view turns slower while aiming.
description=[*][b]Moving[/b]: your character turns to wherever the left stick points and runs there, towards the camera too. Once the right stick rests for a moment, the camera swings in behind you.
description=[*][b]Viewpoint's loot panel[/b] (look at a container, vehicle or body): D-pad Down cycles the rows, RB takes or does the selected one, hold RB to take all. Anywhere else both keep their game function.
description=[*]Menus and radial wheels work as in the game, the camera holds still while they are open.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[table]
description=[tr][th]Xbox[/th][th]PlayStation[/th][th]Steam Deck[/th][/tr]
description=[tr][td]LB / RB[/td][td]L1 / R1[/td][td]L1 / R1[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]LT / RT[/td][td]L2 / R2[/td][td]L2 / R2[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]A / B / X / Y[/td][td]Cross / Circle / Square / Triangle[/td][td]A / B / X / Y[/td][/tr]
description=[/table]
description=The hints in Viewpoint's loot panel follow the button style set in the game's controller options.
description=
description=[b]Recommended preset[/b]: the mod adds [b]Viewpoint Third Person[/b] to Options → Controller → Preset once you have loaded a save with the mod. It is the Default with aiming on LT, shove on LB and rack on D-pad Up, so RT only attacks. Your current preset stays selected until you pick it.
description=
description=With the Original preset, which aims on the right stick, the right stick keeps aiming and the camera does not turn.
description=
description=[b]Steam Deck[/b]: works with the Deck's own controls, no Steam Input layout needed. If you prefer to look with the right trackpad, set it to "As Mouse" in Steam Input, Viewpoint's mouse look works alongside the stick.
description=
