[h1]What it does[/h1]

A dynamic third-person camera for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3809306528]Project Viewpoint[/url], on foot and in vehicles.
Made for [b]mouse and keyboard[/b], with full [b]controller[/b] support.

[list]
[*]Zoom with the mouse wheel, on foot and in vehicles.
[*]Separate camera stances for melee and firearms: wide to watch your flanks, over the shoulder when you aim.
[*]Accuracy stays as the game sets it, by your Aiming skill. This mod does not change it and never will.
[*]Moves in when you enter a room and back out when you leave.
[*]In vehicles it swings in behind you and reacts to how you drive.
[*]Swap shoulders and look around your character with a key, menus free the cursor on their own.
[*]Smooth vehicle motion at high frame rates.
[*]Everything adjustable under Third person in Viewpoint's settings.
[/list]

[h1]Controller[/h1]
[b]First step[/b]: the game starts on the Original preset, which aims with the right stick, so the camera cannot turn. Pick [b]Viewpoint Third Person[/b] (or Default) under Options → Controller → Preset. Viewpoint Third Person shows up there once you have loaded a save with the mod.

Works with the game's own controller layout, on Xbox, PlayStation and Steam Deck controllers. Button names below are Xbox, the table has the others. While Viewpoint's view is on:
[list]
[*][b]Switching views[/b]: hold Back, the button that opens the game's own radial menu, pick the entry with the right stick and let go. Viewpoint on or off, first or third person, swap shoulder.
[*][b]Right stick[/b] turns the camera, on foot and in vehicles. The game aims with it otherwise, so you aim with a light pull on RT, as in the game's Default preset, or with LT in the preset below. The view turns slower while aiming.
[*][b]Moving[/b]: your character turns to wherever the left stick points and runs there, towards the camera too. Once the right stick rests for a moment, the camera swings in behind you.
[*][b]Viewpoint's loot panel[/b] (look at a container, vehicle or body): D-pad Down cycles the rows, RB takes or does the selected one, hold RB to take all. Anywhere else both keep their game function.
[*]Menus and radial wheels work as in the game, the camera holds still while they are open.
[/list]

The hints in Viewpoint's loot panel follow the button style set in the game's controller options.

[b]The Viewpoint Third Person preset[/b] is the Default with aiming on LT, shove on LB and rack on D-pad Up, so RT only attacks.

[b]Steam Deck[/b]: works with the Deck's own controls, no Steam Input layout needed. If you prefer to look with the right trackpad, set it to "As Mouse" in Steam Input, Viewpoint's mouse look works alongside the stick.

[h1]Requirements[/h1]
[list]
[*]PZ Build 42.21 or later
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3809306528]Project Viewpoint[/url]
[/list]

On first launch ZombieBuddy asks whether to load the mod's JAR, tick Allow.

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
[list]
[*]Safe to add or remove mid-save, the mod stores nothing in the save.
[*]First person: the controller support, the loot panel buttons and the cursor key work there too. The camera features are for third person.
[*]Split screen: only player one gets Viewpoint's view, player two stays isometric. This is a limit in Viewpoint.
[*]Aiming from a vehicle is off while Viewpoint's view is on, for now.
[*]Project Viewpoint QOL: some features of Third Person Camera switch off to avoid conflicts. In third person the mouse wheel moves the camera instead of QOL's zoom.
[*]Not meant to run together with Controller Aim for Viewpoint, which changes the controller's aiming, or Project Viewpoint Controller Support, which changes the controller's camera and aiming.
[/list]

Many Viewpoint add-ons hook into the same places, even when they do different things. If something here stops working, another add-on may be the cause. I add compatibility for widely used mods, for the rest please report it.
