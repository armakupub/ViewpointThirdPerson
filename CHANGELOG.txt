Third Person Camera for Project Viewpoint - Changelog
=====================================================

0.1.0 - 2026-10-02
  - First release.
  - On foot: over-the-shoulder camera with smoothed follow, shoulder swap, mouse wheel distance and a closer view indoors.
  - Combat: the combat stance backs off to keep both flanks in view, aiming a firearm moves in over the shoulder.
  - Vehicles: a chase camera with weight, mouse smoothing and speed feel, sized to the vehicle and any trailer.
  - A keyboard key for Viewpoint's cursor toggle, in place of the middle mouse button.
