Third Person Camera for Project Viewpoint - Changelog
=====================================================

0.3.0 - 2026-10-03
  - New defaults after long testing, above all for tight interiors. Settings still at the old defaults switch over once, values you changed stay.
  - New key, unbound by default: look around. While held, the mouse turns the camera round your character, who keeps walking the same way. Letting go or aiming brings it back behind.
  - Better wall collision: the camera pulls in ahead of a wall it is about to meet instead of snapping in, and no longer pumps with each step.
  - Vehicles: the camera follows turns much better at low speed and judders less at high frame rates, above all in turns, trailers included. It can come closer, and a trailer no longer pushes it back.
  - Menus and windows free the cursor and lock it again once closed. With Project Viewpoint QOL loaded, QOL does this.
  - Combat stance and firearm aiming have their own distances indoors and outdoors.
  - New settings: the number of mouse wheel steps, and the loot window key (Tab) always opening your inventory.
  - Head motion follows Viewpoint's Head movement setting. The keys moved to the Third person tab.

0.2.0 - 2026-10-02
  - Controller support, within the game's own layout. While Viewpoint's view is on, the right stick turns the camera, on foot and in vehicles. Aim with a light pull on RT, or with LT in the new preset. The view turns slower while aiming.
  - On foot with a controller the character turns to wherever the left stick points and runs there, towards the camera too. Once the right stick rests, the camera swings in behind.
  - Viewpoint's views switch from the game's Back wheel: Viewpoint on or off, first or third person, swap shoulder.
  - Viewpoint's loot panel with a controller: D-pad Down cycles the rows, RB takes or does the selected one, hold RB to take all. Its hints follow the controller style set in the game's options.
  - New controller preset "Viewpoint Third Person" under Options, Controller, Preset, after a save with the mod has been loaded once: the Default with aiming on LT, shove on LB and rack on D-pad Up.
  - Aiming from a vehicle is off while Viewpoint's view is on. It stood the character up in the seat, jerked the camera and missed the reticle.

0.1.1 - 2026-10-02
  - Fixed: on foot the character looked jittery on screen, above all at lower or uneven frame rates. The camera timed its follow on its own clock while the body moves on the game's, and with uneven frames the two drifted apart.
  - The camera now trails the body by its pace on the game's clock: at a steady pace your character holds still on screen, however uneven the frames. Starting and stopping still lag behind as before.
  - Thanks to everyone who reported it.

0.1.0 - 2026-10-02
  - First release.
  - On foot: over-the-shoulder camera with smoothed follow, shoulder swap, mouse wheel distance and a closer view indoors.
  - Combat: the combat stance backs off to keep both flanks in view, aiming a firearm moves in over the shoulder.
  - Vehicles: a chase camera with weight, mouse smoothing and speed feel, sized to the vehicle and any trailer.
  - A keyboard key for Viewpoint's cursor toggle, in place of the middle mouse button.
