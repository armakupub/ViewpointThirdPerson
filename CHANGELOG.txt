Third Person Camera for Project Viewpoint - Changelog
=====================================================

0.3.9 - 2026-10-08
  - Fixed: jumping, for example with Source Movement for Viewpoint, pulled the camera in to your character. It now keeps its distance in the air, also when jumping down from cars and roofs.
  - New option under On foot: "Zoom in indoors". Turned off, the camera keeps its distance when you go in and out of buildings. On by default.
  - New options under Mouse and keyboard: "Mouse smoothing (s)" and "Mouse speed while aiming", as for the controller and in vehicles. Both off by default.

0.3.8 - 2026-10-06
  - The interact key no longer turns your character to the camera. It works as in vanilla again, on what your character faces.

0.3.7 - 2026-10-06
  - Fixed: the interact key turned your character to the camera while walking, so you could not open a door on the move. It now only turns you while standing still.
  - New option under Mouse and keyboard: "Aiming holds the mouse". With the cursor free, holding the right mouse button aims and turns the camera with the mouse, letting go frees the cursor again. A short right click still opens the context menu. On by default.

0.3.6 - 2026-10-06
  - Fixed: on stairs the camera trailed behind your character. It now climbs with it, while crouching still eases in as before.

0.3.5 - 2026-10-06
  - On foot your character now turns and walks towards the camera instead of backpedalling, and keeps facing where it stopped, also with a controller. The interact key turns it to what the camera looks at. Aiming backpedals as before; the new option "Backpedal when walking backwards" brings back the old walk. Viewpoint's Strafe walking stays as it is.
  - Mouse and keyboard: the camera swings in behind while you walk, 2 s after the mouse rests, as with a controller. "Swing delay" sets the time, 0 turns it off.

0.3.4 - 2026-10-05
  - New option under Mouse and keyboard: "Turn around when walking backwards". Your character turns and walks towards the camera, as with a controller. Off by default.

0.3.3 - 2026-10-05
  - Works with Project Viewpoint QOL again: scrolling in past the nearest step switches to first person.
  - Vehicles: the look-around key holds the view while held.
  - Vehicles: the camera sets its distance per vehicle so the whole rear stays in view, KI5 vehicles included. Distance now adds to that.
  - Vehicles: the camera waits 2 s before swinging in behind.
  - Shorter setting and key texts, some settings renamed.

0.3.2 - 2026-10-05
  - Vehicles: the camera runs smoother, above all in tight turns, and the horizon holds steady. The vehicle, its trailer and the people in it now move in step with the camera, on foot your character too.
  - Speed feel is calmer towards its top end: setting off and changing gear no longer rock the camera. The default feels as before.
  - The camera height follows the vehicle's real roof, so it sits right over tall trucks and vans too, and no longer cuts into the ceiling or jumps while getting in or out.
  - Controller: turning the camera with the right stick is smoother.
  - This reaches deeper into Viewpoint than before. If a Viewpoint update breaks it, the camera works as in 0.3.1 until the next update of this mod.

0.3.1 - 2026-10-04
  - Better compatibility with Viewpoint - Interface and Project Viewpoint QOL.
  - New settings under Mouse and keyboard: "Menus and windows free the cursor" and "Radial menus hide Viewpoint's loot menu".
  - Fixed a thread race in the third-person camera.

0.3.0 - 2026-10-03
  - New defaults after long testing, above all for tight interiors. Settings still at the old defaults switch over once, values you changed stay.
  - New key, unbound by default: look around. While held, the mouse turns the camera round your character, who keeps walking the same way. Letting go or aiming brings it back behind.
  - Better wall collision: the camera pulls in ahead of a wall it is about to meet instead of snapping in, and no longer pumps with each step.
  - Vehicles: the camera follows turns much better at low speed and judders less at high frame rates, above all in turns, trailers included. It can come closer, and a trailer no longer pushes it back.
  - Menus and windows free the cursor and lock it again once closed. With Project Viewpoint QOL loaded, QOL does this.
  - Combat stance and firearm aiming have their own distances indoors and outdoors.
  - New settings: the number of mouse wheel steps, and the loot window key (Tab) always opening your inventory.
  - Head motion follows Viewpoint's Head movement setting. The keys moved to the Third person tab.

0.2.0 - 2026-10-02
  - Controller support, within the game's own layout. While Viewpoint's view is on, the right stick turns the camera, on foot and in vehicles. Aim with a light pull on RT, or with LT in the new preset. The view turns slower while aiming.
  - On foot with a controller the character turns to wherever the left stick points and runs there, towards the camera too. Once the right stick rests, the camera swings in behind.
  - Viewpoint's views switch from the game's Back wheel: Viewpoint on or off, first or third person, swap shoulder.
  - Viewpoint's loot panel with a controller: D-pad Down cycles the rows, RB takes or does the selected one, hold RB to take all. Its hints follow the controller style set in the game's options.
  - New controller preset "Viewpoint Third Person" under Options, Controller, Preset, after a save with the mod has been loaded once: the Default with aiming on LT, shove on LB and rack on D-pad Up.
  - Aiming from a vehicle is off while Viewpoint's view is on. It stood the character up in the seat, jerked the camera and missed the reticle.

0.1.1 - 2026-10-02
  - Fixed: on foot the character looked jittery on screen, above all at lower or uneven frame rates. The camera timed its follow on its own clock while the body moves on the game's, and with uneven frames the two drifted apart.
  - The camera now trails the body by its pace on the game's clock: at a steady pace your character holds still on screen, however uneven the frames. Starting and stopping still lag behind as before.
  - Thanks to everyone who reported it.

0.1.0 - 2026-10-02
  - First release.
  - On foot: over-the-shoulder camera with smoothed follow, shoulder swap, mouse wheel distance and a closer view indoors.
  - Combat: the combat stance backs off to keep both flanks in view, aiming a firearm moves in over the shoulder.
  - Vehicles: a chase camera with weight, mouse smoothing and speed feel, sized to the vehicle and any trailer.
  - A keyboard key for Viewpoint's cursor toggle, in place of the middle mouse button.
